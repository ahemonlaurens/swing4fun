Rules ▶ Show

The Principle: Two teams compete (2v2, 2v1, or 1v3).

The Score (Match): On each hole:

  • With 4 players (2v2): 2 points are in play. The best player of Team 1 faces the best of Team 2 (1 point). The 2nd best players also face off (1 point).
  • With 3 players (2v1 or 1v3): Only 1 point is in play. The solo player compares their score only against the best of the two opposing players.

* In case of a tie in a match, no one scores (0 points). In case of a tie within the same team, the match order is chosen randomly.

The Cross (X): Entering "X" means picking up your ball. This is worth Par + 4. This score is added to your total. A valid score always wins against a Cross (X), even if a team has multiple Crosses. If two crosses face off, it's a perfect tie (0 points).
